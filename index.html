02 / HOW I THINK

Less decoration.
More direction.

My writing process is developing around a simple principle: effective words answer a real question in the buyer’s mind.

01

Understand

Who’s buying? What matters to them? Which objection makes them hesitate?

02

Find a useful angle, lead with the value, and make the claim specific and credible.

03

Write & refine

Build the message, simplify the language, and review the path to action.
